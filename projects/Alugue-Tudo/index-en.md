title: Tool Management System
stack: PHP, Laravel, PostgreSQL, Docker, Tailwind CSS, Livewire
---
System developed for a company to manage tool inventory, employees, and access permissions.

The application provides complete management of tools, employees, roles, and inventory movements, including usage history, change tracking, and role-based access control.

The project was designed to improve internal processes, increase information security, and optimize operational management by digitizing activities that were previously handled manually.
