title: Restaurant Management System
stack: Java 21, Spring Boot, React, PostgreSQL, WebSockets, Docker, JWT, Hexagonal Architecture
---
Complete management system developed for a real client, focused on handling orders, tables, and sales operations in bars and restaurants.

The application was designed to optimize restaurant workflows, providing real-time order management through WebSockets, inventory control with automatic updates based on transactions, sales reports, and secure user management with role-based access control.

The project follows a structured architecture based on **Hexagonal Architecture**, separating business rules, infrastructure, and interfaces to improve maintainability and future scalability.
